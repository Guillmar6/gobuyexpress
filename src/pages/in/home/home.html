About Us

GOBUYEXPRESS is an online shopping website project developed by Team TriNova from Cavite State University Naic, Campus. The website is designed to provide users with a fast, convenient, and user-friendly online shopping experience where customers can browse products, place orders, and manage purchases anytime and anywhere.

The project aims to demonstrate the use of modern web technologies in creating an efficient e-commerce platform that supports product viewing, customer transactions, order management, and secure shopping processes. GOBUYEXPRESS focuses on accessibility, simplicity, and reliability to meet the growing demand for online purchasing services.

Through this project, Team TriNova showcases their skills in web development, database management, system design, and user interface creation while applying the concepts and knowledge learned in the BSIT program.

As part of our academic activity, GOBUYEXPRESS reflects our creativity, teamwork, and dedication in developing an effective and user-friendly e-commerce website.
